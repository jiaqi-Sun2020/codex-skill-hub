# PRL rules used by this skill

Verify all rules against official APS pages before submission.

## Current official principles (checked 2026-09-13)

- PRL publishes short reports of highly important original research with broad physics interest.
- A Letter has a 3,750-word-equivalent core limit.
- Up to two pages of End Matter may be added for specialist material.
- The introduction must state the issue and primary achievement in language accessible across physics subfields.
- Authors should clearly convey impact, innovation, and interest.
- A complete Data Availability Statement is required for published articles.
- Submission includes a compelling justification of approximately 100 words explaining why the work meets PRL criteria.
- APS length estimation includes prose plus word equivalents for displayed mathematics, figures, tables, captions, footnotes, and endnotes.
