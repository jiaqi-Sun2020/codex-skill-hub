# Review title

Adapt this template to the actual review; do not generate empty sections.
Examples and prompts are not facts. Narrative work can omit formal systematic
screening, registration and PRISMA sections; specialized appraisal is conditional.

## Question and scope

State review type, focal question, included source/material range, intended use,
resource bounds and why the selected sources apply.

## Methods and limits

Refer to the canonical review_protocol.md instead of copying its search ledger.
Summarize actual queries/dates/coverage, criteria, amendments, unavailable reports,
reviewer identities/independence and unresolved checks. Supplied-set work discloses
selection; capped search is incomplete, not exhaustive.

## Evidence comparison

Reuse literature_matrix.md where applicable. Locate source versions/passages and
link search records and publication reports to independent studies. Explain
overlapping samples and which report supplies which observation. Keep reporting
completeness, bias concerns and certainty distinct, with applicable rationale.

## Thematic synthesis

For each actual theme/question: supported observation; supporting and contrary
sources; independence and uncertainty; applicability; strongest permissible
inference; unresolved evidence. Do not invent theme counts, statistics or results.

## Discussion and conclusion

Answer the scoped question without overstating novelty, causality or consensus.
Distinguish absence of located evidence from evidence of absence. State review
limitations and the smallest useful next search, design or author decision.

## References and final review

Reuse the active manuscript bibliography and preserve keys. Identity checks,
DOI registration, statement support, correction/version checks and author approval
are separate. Non-DOI sources permit manual verification. Clinical frameworks,
quantitative synthesis, figures, registration and public data release apply only
when justified and authorized; do not expose sensitive or unpublished material.
