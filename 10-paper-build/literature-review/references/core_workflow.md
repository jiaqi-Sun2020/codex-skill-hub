# Review workflow by task

## Scope and protocol

Select narrative, scoping, or systematic work. Record the question, scope,
resource bounds, sources, search dates and eligibility before screening.
For a supplied set, state its selection and do not invent a search history.
PICO is useful for intervention questions, not all research.

## Search and selection

Use appropriate available indexes/manual exports and record exact queries,
filters, date-field meaning, totals, pages exported and failures. Ranked or
capped results do not demonstrate complete coverage. Pilot queries against known
eligible reports. Consult [database_strategies.md](database_strategies.md).

Keep record IDs and report/version IDs separate from study IDs. Record provenance,
duplicate groups, version conflicts, overlapping cohorts and unknown dates.
The local script does not resolve eligibility or independent-study identity.

Systematic work records title/abstract and full-text decisions, exclusion reasons,
reports not retrieved, and real reviewers/conflict resolution. Disclose limited
coverage or single-reviewer assessment; never simulate independent agreement.
Use the appropriate [PRISMA guidance](https://www.prisma-statement.org/) only
for relevant review types. Reconcile records, reports and studies separately.

## Extraction and appraisal

For each consequential claim locate source version and section/page/table, the
reviewed text/result, its uncertainty, independent unit and applicable scope.
Abstract-only evidence cannot stand in for methods/results review. If the
source is unavailable, preserve an explicit gap. Do not infer missing data.

Assess reporting completeness, result-level bias and certainty of the evidence
body separately. Choose a domain tool only when appropriate; consult its current
official guidance before formal use. Tool domains are not an additive generic
quality score. Missing reporting means unknown, not demonstrably not performed.

## Synthesis

Organize by actual questions/themes, not a fixed count or one summary per paper.
Describe support, contradictions, overlap, generalization limits and review
limitations. Separate descriptive, predictive and causal statements. Do not
count several reports of one cohort as replication or combine incompatible
outcomes. Meta-analysis planning can identify what data are needed; execution
requires data-analysis/domain expertise and explicit scope.

## Citation and delivery

Read [search_and_citation.md](search_and_citation.md). Registration is only one
identity check; manually inspect statement support, version and corrections.
Use [review_template.md](../assets/review_template.md) only when synthesis is
requested. Reuse the active manuscript bibliography; do not manufacture keys or
rewrite it without approval. PDF/LaTeX assembly and figures have separate owners.

Maintain one review_protocol.md for searches, decisions, study/report links,
appraisal and remaining gaps. The matrix holds comparison rows; the synthesis
holds the narrative. No separate ledger per gate.
